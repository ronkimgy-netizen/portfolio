01 — Overview

About this project

SAM.gov is the U.S. federal government's official system for finding federal contract opportunities, grants, and entity records. Unlike the specialized analyst tools in my other case studies, SAM.gov serves a broad public audience, including vendors, researchers, journalists, and small business owners, who typically arrive with a clear goal in mind but with different mental models of how federal data is organized.

This case study examines a core information architecture challenge:

how search results are filtered and categorized across multiple domains, and how the current taxonomy information hierarchy can better align with how users actually think about what they're looking for.
