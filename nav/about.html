About Me

Ron Kim

I am a UI/UX Designer based in Washington, D.C., specializing in transforming complex data-driven systems into clear, usable designs for web, mobile, and interactive platforms, as well as for technology and people. Over the years, I have proven my ability to design visual systems, data visualizations, and interfaces for large-scale platforms serving a wide range of users, including those with limited digital access. Dedicated to human-centered design to help solve real-world problems, such as improving federal infrastructure and public services. Strong background in visual design, building design systems, and working closely with developers and researchers.
